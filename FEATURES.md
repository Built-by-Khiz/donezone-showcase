# Features

## Shape your workspace

Create boards and lists, rename them as work changes, duplicate a list with or without its tasks, and lock a list when you want its layout to stay put. Desktop users can drag lists to reorder them.

## Keep the next action clear

Tasks hold a title, notes, due date and time, completion state, and color labels. Create, edit, duplicate, copy, move, archive, or delete tasks from their menus. Task movement is available through both desktop dragging and menu controls.

Due dates determine card order, with undated work last. Red highlights overdue work, amber highlights the next 24 hours, and green highlights the coming week. Click a date to edit it.

## See only what matters

Give labels meaningful names and colors. Click any card label to filter the board, then clear the filter to see everything again. Archived tasks and lists remain available for restoration.

## Keep a recovery point

Save board snapshots and restore an earlier version. Automatic backup settings support daily, interval, weekly, and monthly recurrence while a board is open. These browser-based schedules do not run unattended when the app is closed.

## Make yourself at home

Email magic-link sign-in opens your saved workspace. A browser-memory demo lets you explore without creating an account. Light and dark themes, responsive layouts, and Plus Jakarta Sans keep the experience readable.
